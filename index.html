⚽ Basic Soccer Rules

  • 11 players on each team (including the goalkeeper)
  • The ball can only be kicked or headed — no hands (except the goalkeeper!)
  • A game is usually 90 minutes long (two halves of 45 minutes)
  • You score a goal by getting the ball into the other team's net
  • If the ball goes out of bounds on the side → throw-in
  • If it goes out behind the goal → goal kick or corner kick
  • No pushing, tripping, or being mean — that's a foul
  • Yellow card = warning • Red card = you're out of the game!

🌍 Soccer Around the World

Soccer (or "football" in most countries) is the most popular sport on Earth!

  • Brazil – 5 World Cup wins. Home of the "beautiful game"
  • Germany – 4 World Cups. Known for teamwork and strong defense
  • Argentina – Home of Messi. Passionate fans everywhere
  • France – Won the World Cup in 2018. Lots of fast, skilled players
  • England – Where modern soccer began. Home of the Premier League
  • Spain – Famous for "tiki-taka" – lots of short, quick passes
  • USA – Growing fast! Strong women's team and exciting MLS
  • Japan & South Korea – Super disciplined and technical players

💡 Did you know? Over 250 million people play soccer around the world!

🗣️ Cool Soccer Words

Goal – When the ball goes into the net!
Assist – A pass that helps a teammate score
Hat-trick – Scoring 3 goals in one game
Clean sheet – When the goalkeeper doesn't let any goals in
Dribble – Running with the ball using your feet
Pass – Kicking the ball to a teammate
Tackle – Taking the ball away from an opponent
Offside – Being too far forward when the ball is passed
Corner – A kick from the corner of the field
Free kick – A kick awarded after a foul
Penalty – A special free kick from the penalty spot
Stadium – The big place where games are played

🧮 Soccer Math Fun

Practice your math with soccer!

If a team scores 3 goals in the first half and 2 in the second half, how many goals did they score in total?

5 goals! ⚽

A game is 90 minutes. How many minutes is each half?

45 minutes each half

If Messi scores 2 goals in 4 games, how many goals does he average per game?

0.5 goals per game (2 ÷ 4)

There are 11 players on a team. How many players are on the field if both teams are playing?

22 players!
